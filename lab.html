Practice Lab Studio / Case study + demo

AI-drafted training guides for datacenter trainers.

I built this at Microsoft to turn training-needs assessments and operating procedures into a guide for datacenter trainers. AI combined the source material into a draft, and automation assembled the document for review.

The real product

The case study

Datacenter trainers needed to prepare staff for new technologies. The assessments explained what people needed to learn. Engineering and execution teams’ procedures explained the work. Someone still had to bring those documents together and write a guide another trainer could follow.

I built the workflow in Copilot Studio and Power Automate. AI used the combined source data to draft the topics, safety callouts, tooling, practice, and training completion checks. Automation assembled the document. I shared and demonstrated the working version, then redesigned the workflow for production needs.

Why consistency mattered

The guide was a global standard document for training across hundreds of datacenters. Trainers needed consistent topics, safety callouts, tools, and completion checks. I worked on the prompts, document fields, review, and delivery as well as the workflow. That scope describes the document, not a rollout of the tool to every site.

The demo beside this story uses fictional documents and a prepared draft so you can try the workflow without internal access. You can edit the guide, check it against both inputs, and download Word. It doesn’t run live AI or connect to a datacenter.

Project details and optional planning model ↗

Source-grounded generative AI workflow

This outline follows the public architecture and blueprints. It describes the redesign, not an export of the original internal system.

Is it RAG?

With connected source retrieval, it follows the RAG pattern: retrieve reference material, then use it to ground the AI’s draft. This repo leaves the reader unconfigured and also supports pasted text, so these files don’t confirm retrieval in the original system. No vector index or semantic-search implementation is specified here.

The browser demo is separate: bundled fictional sources, a prepared guide, and a local Word generator. It doesn’t run these prompts or flows, or perform live RAG.
