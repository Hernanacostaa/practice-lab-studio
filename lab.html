The decisions behind the demo
Useful is more than “it generated text.”

      

        
01 / Start with facts
No guide? No guessing.
The workflow stops when the source is unavailable. Missing facts stay TBD. Open “What if the guide is missing?” to try the failure path yourself.

        
02 / Keep edits deliberate
Your change stays your change.
Editing one field preserves the others. A preview shows applied changes, not unfinished typing, and every change clears the previous approval.

        
03 / Put people in control
Review is a real step.
A draft is not a finished lesson. Downloads stay locked until a person reviews the current version. Pending edits cannot slip into an approved handoff.

        
04 / Deliver something usable
Not trapped in a chat.
The same 17 fields carry through to the Word document and JSON file. No extra generation happens during export, and this public demo works offline.
